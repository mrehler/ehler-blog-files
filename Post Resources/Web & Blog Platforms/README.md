In my post [Some Thoughts on Blogging](https://mrehler.com/2024/01/10/some-thoughts-on-blogging/), I mentioned a few different small platforms for eking out your own place on the web. Such a list obviously stands to never be complete, but I wanted to be able to add to it.[^note]

## Blogging Platforms

- [Posthaven](https://posthaven.com)
    - $5/mo
    - Main feature:archival mode so posts never get deleted
- [Blot](https://blot.im/)
    - $6/mo
    - all about being a folder to drag into
- [Pika](pika.page)
    - $6/mo or $60/yr
- [public.me](https://public.me/blog)
    - iMessage blogging. Free
- [Notepin.co](https://Notepin.co)
    - Free with upgrades
    - Gates RSS behind those upgrades though
- Wordpress.com (obvious)
- [Bear Blog](https://bearblog.dev/) for free (I believe)



## Other novel ‘site’ platforms
(Not explicitly blogging platforms)

- <https://straw.page/>
- [Neocities](https://neocities.org/) for free or low cost
- [Blot](https://blot.im/)
